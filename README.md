## About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **Middle East Technical University**, with experience in full-stack development, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and AI-related research.

I like building things that go beyond prototypes, whether that's working on the architecture behind an application, figuring out how different services communicate, or bringing an unusual idea to life.

- 📱 Co-founder of **[Deviny](https://github.com/Deviny-me)**, a fitness and social platform connecting users with trainers and nutritionists.
- 🎧 Developing an **adaptive reading and listening system** that analyzes narrative mood, pacing, and context to synchronize music and ambience with the story.
- 🔬 **Third co-author of a medical AI research publication** involving Stanford Medicine and the Hasso Plattner Institute.
- 🧠 Currently exploring **Hugging Face Transformers and LangChain**, with an interest in applied AI and software architecture.

## Featured Projects

### 🩺 [MedAnnotateApp](https://github.com/ArkhunAbdullazade/MedAnnotateApp)

A medical image annotation platform I developed as part of an AI-for-medicine research collaboration involving Stanford Medicine and the Hasso Plattner Institute.

Built role-based workflows for medical students and professionals, including visual annotation tools, clinical-term highlighting, filtered image assignment, timing capture, and image locking for controlled research studies.

**Stack:** `.NET 8` `ASP.NET Core MVC` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

**[View repository, screenshots & video walkthrough →](https://github.com/ArkhunAbdullazade/MedAnnotateApp)**

<sub>Demonstrations show an earlier local development version.</sub>

### 🎵 [Spotibuds](https://github.com/Spotibuds)

A microservice-based social music platform combining music discovery with social features.

My main contributions included user profiles, social relationships, real-time messaging, notifications, and activity feeds, alongside shared work on the Music and Identity services, frontend, and Docker setup.

**Stack:** `.NET 8` `MongoDB` `SignalR` `RabbitMQ` `Azure`

**[View organization & feature demonstrations →](https://github.com/Spotibuds)**

### 📖 [MangaFlex](https://github.com/ArkhunAbdullazade/MangaFlex)

A team-built manga and comics platform powered by MangaDex, bringing together title discovery, chapter reading, reading history, communities, and real-time chats.

**Stack:** `ASP.NET Core MVC` `PostgreSQL` `MediatR` `xUnit`

**[View repository →](https://github.com/ArkhunAbdullazade/MangaFlex)**

### 💪 [Deviny](https://github.com/Deviny-me)

A fitness-focused social platform I'm co-founding and developing. It connects users with trainers and nutritionists through messaging, scheduling, program purchases, and professional service-management tools.

**[View organization →](https://github.com/Deviny-me)**

<sub>Active commercial development; most repositories are private.</sub>
